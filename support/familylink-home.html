Frequently Asked Questions

How do I invite family members?

Share your family code with the people you want to invite. They can use the code to join your family group.

My calendar is not syncing

Check that your device has an internet connection and that you are signed in to the correct family account.

Can everyone create events?

Family members can use the shared calendar to keep the family updated with appointments and activities.

Notifications are not working

Check that notifications are enabled for FamilyLink Home in your device settings.

Can I use FamilyLink Home on multiple devices?

Yes. Sign in with your account to access your family information on supported devices.
