Frequently Asked Questions

How do I ask TutorHjælp a question?

Type your question in the chat or take a picture of your homework to get help with the assignment.

Does TutorHjælp give me the answer?

TutorHjælp is designed to help you learn. It guides you step by step with explanations and hints instead of simply giving you the final answer.

Can I take a picture of my homework?

Yes. You can use the camera to photograph an assignment and let TutorHjælp help you understand the problem.

Speech is not working

Check that sound is enabled on your device and that TutorHjælp has the necessary permissions.

What subjects can TutorHjælp help with?

TutorHjælp can assist with several school subjects including mathematics, Danish, English, geography, biology, physics and German.

Is AI always correct?

No. Artificial intelligence can make mistakes. TutorHjælp should be used as a learning assistant, and important information should always be checked.
