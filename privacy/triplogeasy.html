Privacy Policy

TripLog Easy
Last updated: June 2026

TripLog Easy respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal data.

Information We Collect

TripLog Easy may collect and store the following information on your device:

  • Location data used to record trips and mileage.
  • Photos and screenshots selected by the user as documentation for expenses and receipts.
  • Work time entries entered by the user.
  • Mileage records, trip information, notes, expenses, and reimbursement calculations.
  • App settings and preferences.

How We Use Information

The information is used solely to:

  • Record and manage mileage logs.
  • Generate driving reports and reimbursement documentation.
  • Store expense documentation.
  • Calculate work hours and salary estimates.
  • Generate PDF reports for the user.

Data Storage

All data is stored locally on the user's device.

TripLog Easy does not upload, sell, rent, or share user data with third parties.

Location Data

Location access is used only for automatic trip detection and mileage tracking.

Location data remains on the user's device unless the user chooses to export a report.

Photos and Receipts

Photos selected by the user are stored locally on the device and are only used as documentation for expenses and reimbursements.

Data Sharing

TripLog Easy does not share personal information with advertisers, analytics providers, or third parties.

Data Deletion

Users can delete trips, expenses, work records, and other stored data directly within the app.

Deleting the app removes all locally stored data.

Children's Privacy

TripLog Easy is not intended for children under the age of 13.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Changes will be reflected in the latest version available through the app.
